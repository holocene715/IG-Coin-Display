This bronze coin was struck at **Antioch on the Orontes**, one of the principal cities and mints of the Seleucid Empire, during the joint rule of **Cleopatra Thea and her son Antiochos VIII Grypos**.

The radiate portrait of Antiochos VIII occupies the obverse, while the reverse depicts an **owl standing on an amphora**. The surrounding Greek inscription names both Cleopatra and Antiochos, reflecting their joint authority during this period.

The coin dates to the final decades of the Seleucid Empire, when dynastic disputes increasingly divided a kingdom that had once extended across much of the Near East and Central Asia.
