This billon tetradrachm was issued under **Gondophares I**, founder and one of the best-known rulers of the Indo-Parthian Kingdom. His realm developed across territories encompassing parts of present-day Afghanistan, Pakistan, and northwestern India during the first century CE.

The coin illustrates the cultural mixture characteristic of the Indo-Parthian world. Its obverse presents Gondophares in a portrait tradition derived from Hellenistic coinage and surrounds him with a **Greek inscription**, while the reverse depicts **Nike**, the Greek personification of victory, alongside an inscription in **Kharosthi script**.

The type is attributed to **Arachosia**, an ancient region centered on what is now southern Afghanistan. The combination of Greek imagery and language with Kharosthi reflects the overlapping Hellenistic, Iranian, and South Asian traditions that characterized coinage in this region.
